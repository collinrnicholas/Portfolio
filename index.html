Portfolio strategy · Persado

Connecting acquisition, adoption, and retention into one product growth strategy.

Marketing intelligence, opportunity development, customer value, and enterprise expansion all sit on the same commercial chain. Each product removes a different reason a customer stalls: nothing to change, no way to act, no proof it worked.

See the portfolio in detail →
Acquisition · Identify opportunities

Find the accounts worth pursuing

  • Challenge: acquisition and win-back effort went to accounts that were never going to convert.
  • Decision: score marketing assets for conversion and compliance evidence, then turn each finding into a personalized outreach draft.
  • My role: Market Scan direction, which was live before I joined. The prospecting engine was my idea, built end to end.
  • Status: Market Scan live across the customer base; prospecting in outbound rollout.
Adoption · Activate value

Turn intelligence into action

  • Challenge: teams had recommendations but no repeatable path from finding to change.
  • Decision: put prioritized opportunities in front of the marketer with the evidence attached, before the change rather than after.
  • My role: product direction for the workflow and the prioritization logic behind it.
  • Status: live; adoption measured through accounts and assets reviewed.
Retention · Prove impact

Make customer value visible

  • Challenge: customers experienced Persado through our team, not the product, so value went unseen at renewal.
  • Decision: send scheduled value summaries into accounts and into business units we don't serve yet.
  • My role: my idea and the first build, shipped to production with engineering.
  • Status: in production; used in QBRs and monthly touchpoints.

Executive leadership

How
I lead.

Product leadership means making the right decisions, building alignment around them, and creating an organization capable of delivering the outcome.

More about how I think →
01

Set direction

Turn business goals, customer evidence, and commercial opportunity into prioritized direction.At Jobvite, I set the shared-service strategy: one integration, three products, one commercial model.

02

Allocate investment

Decide where to invest, where to validate demand, and where not to build.At Employ, I tested application fraud demand with an AI-built MVP before engineering committed: 300+ registrations, 20–40 inbound demos.

03

Build organizations

Create alignment across product, design, engineering, and go-to-market.At Persado, I rebuilt how the team ships: weekly releases, a first roadmap, and launch enablement none of which existed before.

04

Own outcomes

Connect product decisions to revenue, retention, adoption, or operational improvement.At Employ, a recovery I led turned a churn risk into a two-year, $880K agreement at 1.6× ARR.

I directly lead product and the design function at Persado, and I lead through influence everywhere else: engineering, revenue, marketing, customer success, and external partners. Both matter. One ships the product; the other decides whether it reaches the customer.
