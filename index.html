How I Operate

Three principles I run product on

These decide what gets funded, what gets killed, and what I am willing to put my name on.

01

Ship the artefact, not the deck

A working prototype settles in one demo what three weeks of PRD debate cannot. I get something real in front of customers and executives fast, then invest on observed signal instead of internal consensus.

02

Platforms compound; features expire

The durable wins are structural: one integration serving three platforms, one pricing model covering five capabilities, one design language across 1,500 customers. I hunt for the decision that makes the next ten cheaper.

03

Revenue is the proof

Strategy without a number attached is a hypothesis. Every initiative I lead has a pricing model, a monetization path, and an accountable owner defined before build starts — not bolted on after launch.
