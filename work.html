Portfolio strategy · Persado

Growth, retention, and product design.

Director of Product since August 2026. Growth and retention product for enterprise financial services, plus the product design function. Three products, one commercial chain: find the opportunity, act on it, prove the value.

Operating model

Weekly releases, up from every few months

With engineering leadership: stand-ups, simplified Jira workflows, the team's first roadmap, and a weekly Wednesday deploy. That moved the constraint into marketing and enablement, which is why release messaging now goes out in Slack and email and enablement sessions cover sales, marketing, and customers. Read the case →

Acquisition

Market Scan and agentic prospecting

Market Scan reviews live and planned marketing content for conversion performance and compliance risk. It launched before August 2026; product direction is mine. The prospecting engine was my idea and my build: it crawls the sites of roughly 800 U.S. banks, scores their assets with Market Scan, and turns findings into personalized outreach drafts. The campaign it powers is running now.

Adoption

Turning analysis into action

Prioritized opportunities reach the marketer with the evidence attached, before the change rather than after. The decision was to make the recommendation inspectable — performance and compliance evidence in view — so teams can act without re-litigating the analysis.

Retention & expansion

ROI & Value Dashboard

Most customers experience Persado through our team rather than the product, so delivered value often goes unseen at renewal. The first version of a dashboard turned experiment results into scheduled value summaries for quarterly business reviews. The bigger win is expansion: the same summary goes to business units we don't serve yet — here's what Persado did for your credit card team.
