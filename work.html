Operating model

Weekly releases, up from every few months

Friction between product and engineering meant releases went out every few months. With engineering leadership, I introduced stand-ups, simplified Jira workflows, the team's first roadmap, and a weekly Wednesday deploy. That moved the constraint into marketing and enablement, so I added release messaging in Slack and email and now lead enablement meetings for sales, marketing, and customers. None of it existed before.

Asset intelligence

Market Scan

I lead Market Scan, an AI product used across the customer base. It reviews live and planned marketing content for conversion performance and compliance risk, then prioritizes optimization opportunities. The product was live when I joined.

Acquisition

Agentic prospecting

My idea, built end to end. The engine crawls the sites of roughly 800 U.S. banks, scores their marketing assets with Market Scan, and turns the findings into personalized outreach drafts. I'm now running the outbound campaign it powers.

Customer value

ROI & Value Dashboard

Most customers experience Persado through our team rather than the product, so the value we deliver often goes unseen. I built the first version of a dashboard that turns experiment results into value summaries, sent weekly, monthly, or quarterly and used in QBRs and monthly touchpoints. The bigger win is expansion. We usually serve one business line per customer, so we send the same summary to other business units: here's what Persado did for your credit card team.
