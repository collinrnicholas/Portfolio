What the progression means

The pattern across all five.

Support taught me what breaks. Consulting taught me how customers actually use what they buy. Program leadership taught me how to move an organization that does not report to me. Product taught me that all three are the same job: deciding where investment earns its place, then proving it.

The through-line is commercial. A shared service priced as a product, a recovery that ended in expansion, a delivery model that made experimentation possible — each one is a decision about where the company's money goes next.
