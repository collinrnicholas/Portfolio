The PM Toolkit.
Decisions that hold up.

A grounded AI workspace connecting discovery, cited research, strategy, and delivery around a shared product context. Sources and confidence indicators make the reasoning easier to review.

Role
Founder · Product, pricing & engineering
Status
Live product
Focus
Discovery → delivery
01 · The problem

Keep the reasoning connected to the plan.

Product discovery creates insights that need to carry into research, strategy, and delivery. When those outputs are disconnected, the reasoning behind a plan becomes harder to inspect.

The product starts with that need: keep the customer context and supporting evidence available as the work moves into a planning artifact.

02 · The product decision

Attach the evidence to the artifact, not to the person.

Research includes sources and confidence indicators. Editable artifacts let a product manager inspect the reasoning and correct an assumption before using the output.

The workflow connects discovery, research, and strategy to PRDs, stories, and other delivery documents around the same product context.

The tradeoff is deliberate: make room to review the evidence and confidence behind an output before treating it as a product decision.

03 · What it took

Make the decision tangible.

I designed, built, and shipped the product independently. Working through the experience makes decisions about scope, context, and artifact quality concrete.

That practice informs my product leadership: put a working interaction and its underlying assumptions in front of people so the tradeoff can be discussed and tested.

04 · The value

A connected planning workflow.

The PM Toolkit is a live product spanning discovery, research, strategy, and delivery documents. A product manager can review the reasoning and carry context into the next artifact.

Product output. Research with visible sources and confidence, connected planning documents, and editable deliverables.

Inspect the product for yourself.
