Home / Work / Arclo Prospecting

Deterministic facts, generated prose.

Arclo Prospecting is an agentic system for local-SEO outreach. It discovers local businesses, audits their websites, scores how good a prospect each one is, and drafts a personalized email grounded in what it actually found — and it will not let a model assert a fact it cannot point at evidence for.

01 · The problem

Cold outreach fails on evidence, not on volume

A local-SEO agency’s best leads are businesses with a real demand signal and a broken or absent web presence — a dental practice with sixty Google reviews and no website, or one with a site that has no service pages, no schema, and no mobile viewport.

Finding those businesses by hand does not scale, and the industry’s answer — send more generic email — makes the problem worse. The pitch that converts is specific: “here are the five gaps we found on your site, and here is what each one is costing you.” Producing that pitch at a hundred leads a week is the actual product problem.

That framing set the requirement the system is built around: every claim in an outgoing email has to be traceable to something the system observed.

02 · The governing rule

Deterministic code produces facts. The model produces prose.

The single decision that shaped the whole system: a language model is never the source of a fact. HTTP status codes, schema presence, service-page coverage, viewport configuration — all of that is computed by deterministic code. The model’s only jobs are the scan summary and the outreach email, and it writes them exclusively from findings that already exist.

Every finding carries machine-readable evidence attached to it. A finding without evidence does not ship — it fails validation before it can reach a draft. That constraint is what makes the output defensible: when a prospect replies “what do you mean my site has no service pages,” there is a stored, checkable answer rather than a plausible sentence.

A finding without evidence does not ship.

This is also a governance position, not just an engineering one. It is the same discipline I apply to AI products in an enterprise setting: the model is allowed to be fluent, and it is not allowed to be the system of record.

03 · Architecture

Crawling a hundred websites is not a request

The structural decision that mattered most was separating the web application from the work. Fetching a hundred dental sites, running analysis on each, and calling a model is minutes-to-hours of processing that has to survive a deploy, a timeout, and a crash — none of which a request-scoped handler survives.

So the app never crawls anything inline. It writes a row to a jobs table and returns. A separate long-running worker claims jobs atomically, takes a cooperative lock, executes, and records results.

Web app
Next.js + TypeScript on the App Router. UI and HTTP only. Holds the public domain, runs migrations, never touches a model.
Worker
A separate Node process from the same image. Claims jobs, owns the LLM keys, no port and no HTTP surface.
Queue
Postgres, not Redis. At this scale Postgres is the right queue, and it removes an entire service from the ops surface.
Failure model
Per-job, with attempts, backoff, and terminal state. One dead website cannot fail a campaign of a hundred.
Liveness
A heartbeat row written every two seconds, so “nothing is running” and “nothing is happening” are distinguishable on screen rather than both looking like a stuck page.
04 · The pipeline

Six job types, one gate

The pipeline is six job types rather than one large agent, because each stage has an explicit input, an explicit output, and can be retried on its own. A hundred-prospect campaign is a fan-out of roughly six hundred small jobs.

Discover
Find local businesses in a radius and classify each into a track.
Crawl & analyze
Fetch the site, then run the deterministic rule registry to emit findings with evidence.
Score
Versioned weights across ICP fit, SEO gap, and Arclo fit. Weights are versioned so a scoring change is auditable rather than silent.
Summarize
The model writes the summary, citing findings by id. Nothing enters the prose that is not already a finding.
Human gate
Every prospect stops at Needs Review. A person approves or rejects before a draft is even generated — and approves the draft before it counts as outreach.
05 · The judgement call

The smaller group is the better lead

Discovery splits prospects into two tracks that are genuinely different products rather than two variants of one. Most have a website; a minority have none at all.

The instinct is to treat the no-website group as the weaker list, since there is no site to audit and therefore nothing to say. The opposite is true. Measured on a hundred dentists in Washington DC, 88 landed in the site-gap track and 12 had no site at all. A practice with sixty Google reviews and nowhere to send that demand has already proven the demand — and the offer, “we will build your site before you pay anything,” is a direct fit. It is the strongest lead in the list.

The concrete failure this design prevents: a no-website prospect must never be queued for a crawl. If it were, the crawl returns nothing, findings are empty, the summary has nothing to describe, and the result is a row that looks processed in the dashboard but is completely unusable. That is why the track is a stored, indexed field rather than a condition open-coded in each handler — a lesson learned the expensive way on the first run.

06 · What it proves

An agentic system that can be trusted with a customer’s name on it

Arclo is the clearest expression of how I think agentic products should be built. Autonomy where it is cheap and reversible, and a hard human gate exactly where the system speaks to someone outside the building. The model is used for what it is actually good at — turning structured facts into fluent, specific prose — and constrained everywhere else.

It also demonstrates the part most AI product plans get wrong: the unglamorous infrastructure is the product. The queue, the job state machine, the heartbeat, the versioned weights, and the evidence contract are what make the output trustworthy. The model is one component.
