Arclo Prospecting.
Evidence before prose.

An internal system for local-business discovery, website analysis, and outreach drafting. Structured findings preserve the evidence and uncertainty behind a draft.

Role
Product lead · Owned end to end
Scope
Discovery → evidence → draft
Product boundary
Separate from the Arclo Pro service
01 · The problem

A specific message needs a checkable observation.

A missing page, an incomplete crawl, and an unverified service need different treatment. The product challenge was to preserve those distinctions as website observations became an outreach draft.

The requirement was to connect each claim to a stored observation and keep uncertainty visible. A finding needs enough context for a person to understand what was observed and what remains unknown.

02 · The governing decision

Separate the observation from the writing.

Structured checks produce findings before generation. The model works from those findings, while the source remains attached to the record for review.

  1. 01 · DiscoverFind and classify local businesses.
  2. 02 · ObserveRead the site and store findings with the evidence behind each one.
  3. 03 · DraftGenerate copy from the findings and preserve its provenance.
  4. 04 · ReviewA person inspects the evidence and the draft before anything is sent.

The tradeoff is a narrower claim when coverage is incomplete. Keeping that limit visible makes the evidence easier to inspect before outreach is approved.

03 · What it took

Work that takes minutes has to survive a failure.

Website analysis can fail through timeouts, access restrictions, or unreadable pages. The workflow needs to preserve what was observed and make an incomplete stage visible.

Persisted job state separates the web interface from the worker that performs analysis and generation. Attempts, retries, and terminal outcomes give failures a recovery path.

Discovery
Find and classify local businesses by market.
Evidence store
Every finding keeps its source page and its level of certainty.
Recovery
Persisted outcomes and retry handling make incomplete stages inspectable.
Review boundary
Drafting and sending are separate states. Nothing leaves without a person deciding it should.
04 · The product boundary

Keep the service and the acquisition tool distinct.

Arclo Pro is the customer-facing website and ongoing SEO service. This case describes a separate internal prospecting system that supports acquisition.

The product rule is to preserve the finding, source, and uncertainty through the workflow. Evidence review and manual dispatch remain separate from draft generation.

05 · The value

Reviewable evidence. Reviewable drafts.

The deliverable is a prospect record connecting the business, website findings, source evidence, and draft. A reviewer can trace the statement back to the observation.

Product output. Structured findings and outreach drafts with their evidence available for review before manual dispatch.
