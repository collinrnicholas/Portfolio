BidPacket. Automate the assembly, never the judgment.

Turn construction plans and RFPs into a project summary, materials checklist, and editable proposal draft. The contractor reviews the scope, quantities, pricing, and final submission.

Role
Founder · Product, pricing & support
Status
Live product
Boundary
Contractor verifies the final bid
01 · The bet

A first pass contractors can trust.

Bid preparation starts with understanding the plans or RFP, organizing the scope, and resolving open questions. My bet: contractors will trust AI to assemble a bid packet if it never pretends to price the job.

02 · The product decision

Assembly is automated. Judgment stays with the contractor.

The output is a project summary, a materials checklist, and an editable proposal draft, with open questions visible for review.

The experience is scoped around preparation and review. Quantities, prices, terms, and the final submission stay with the contractor; the packet is a starting point for that judgment.

03 · The workflow

Documents in. A reviewable packet out.

  1. 01 · UnderstandStart with the generated project summary and reconcile it against the source documents.
  2. 02 · OrganizeReview the materials checklist and items flagged for confirmation.
  3. 03 · PrepareEdit the proposal draft, verify quantities and prices, and confirm the terms before submission.
