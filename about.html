Approach

Connect strategy, design, and delivery.

At Persado, I lead growth and retention products for enterprise financial services and directly manage the design team. Market Scan, agentic prospecting, and the ROI & Value Dashboard connect acquisition opportunities with the value customers can see from their experiments.

I reshaped how we develop and deliver products at Persado, rebuilding collaboration with engineering so teams can release earlier. That operating-model work connects product direction with the relationships and delivery practices needed to execute it.

At Employ, I managed relationships with Twilio, ID.me, VONQ, and ProboTalent. Twilio was the largest, representing $600K+ in spend. That partnership scope sits alongside experience launching products with GTM and Revenue and leading enterprise rollouts.

I use AI-assisted prototyping to make a product decision tangible. A working interaction gives customers and teams something specific to respond to before a larger engineering commitment.

A systems perspective

I trained in mechanical engineering. I bring that systems perspective to product strategy, pricing and packaging, prioritization, and product design: understand how the parts interact, test the assumptions, and connect the roadmap to customer and commercial outcomes.
