What I do

I own AI product direction across three applicant tracking systems at Employ Inc. — Jobvite, Lever, and TalentFit — serving 27,800+ enterprise customers. That work runs from competitive and vendor evaluation through platform architecture, SKU pricing, and the go-to-market motion that lands it with customers.

What sets me apart is that I do not stop at managing AI products. I build with AI. I was the first at Employ to adopt vibe coding as a prototyping methodology, trained 15 product and design team members on it, and independently shipped a full subscription SaaS product in production on my own time.

Before Employ, I spent six years at MicroStrategy, progressing from product support through Principal Technical Product Manager to Product Owner — generating $31M in pipeline, training 500+ consultants worldwide, and owning the HyperIntelligence mobile product. That is where I learned how enterprise software is actually sold, deployed, and supported, which is the part most product portfolios skip.

How I work

I lead with a working artefact. A prototype in front of a customer settles more in one session than a month of internal alignment, so I get to something real fast and then invest on what I observed. It also changes the room: when product can demonstrate instead of describe, engineering conversations get shorter and better.

I look for structural wins — the shared service that makes three integrations cheap instead of one, the pricing model that covers five capabilities, the design language that survives 1,500 customers. Features expire. Platforms compound. And I define the number before build starts, because strategy without a number attached is a hypothesis.

What I’m looking for

I am exploring senior product leadership roles — VP or CPO-track — focused on AI/ML products, platform strategy, and developer tools. I want scope over several products or a platform, a mandate to define how AI shows up in the business, and a team that ships.
